    A deliberately narrow job

    Make expert findings reviewable

    This is not a chatbot or an automated professional verdict. It is a legible chain of evidence and judgment for an expert-led review.

    01

    Trace each finding

    Connect every material finding to declared evidence. Missing support and scope limits remain visible.

    02

    Preserve the expert gate

    An evaluation can pass while a qualified domain specialist's decision is pending. The records never collapse those states.

    03

    Prove the handoff

    The handoff names an approved finding set and an exact commit, artifact digest, workflow run, actor, time, and result.

    The same record spine can support research synthesis, engineering assurance, policy review, due diligence, technical advisory work, or regulated document review. Each domain still owns its sources, terminology, rubric, professional qualifications, and final decision.
