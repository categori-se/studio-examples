# Fictional service-access planning record

This fixture describes a hypothetical regional service-access review. It is not
an actual public-service record, operating recommendation, or funding decision.

The fictional usage summary identifies three candidate service locations. North
District has a complete usage extract and readiness checklist. River Ward is
missing one accessibility inspection, and Hill Centre still requires field
verification of the reported opening-hours constraint.

The bounded finding supported by this fixture is that North District is ready
for field review while River Ward must remain qualified. The fixture does not
support a final prioritization, funding decision, service-quality judgment, or
claim about people who are absent from the declared record.
